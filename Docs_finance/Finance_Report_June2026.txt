4. RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
a) Protect the Pechopak + Leg unli business. Half your revenue is
   4 items. Never run out of whole chicken on weekends — June 21
   (a Saturday, PHP 14.3k) shows weekend peak is nearly 4x the
   slowest weekday. Buy chicken against a weekend forecast, not
   flat per-day amounts.

b) Chicken/pork is your #2 cost (PHP 46.9k). You buy from Sadia
   and Seara in small 11–24 kg boxes many times a month, plus
   emergency "Raw Chicken" buys (PHP 1,000–4,200 on June 10 —
   likely a stock-out). Consolidating into 1–2 scheduled bulk
   orders per week should get a better per-kilo price and avoid
   panic buying.

c) Loans are eating 26% of profit. PHP 16.3k/month goes to the
   60k loan (10 payments left), Atome, and HomeCredit. If sales
   hold, prioritize killing the smallest loan first to free up
   monthly cash. Avoid new installment purchases until at least
   one is cleared.

d) Tighten cash controls:
   - Log every expense with a description (no more "Unknown").
   - Move staff cash advances to a fixed weekly cap and record
     them against each person's payroll — small daily CAs (50–500)
     are scattered through the ledger and easy to lose track of.

e) Slow-day promo: Tuesdays/Wednesdays (June 3 = PHP 3.8k) drag
   the average down. A weekday-only offer on Wings or BBQ (your
   cheaper proteins, currently underperforming) fills slow days
   without discounting the star Pechopak items.

f) Push drinks attachment. Icetea is high-margin and already sells
   well (PHP 14.6k). Train staff to offer a pitcher upgrade on
   every group dine-in table — pitcher (129) vs 4 glasses (156)
   feels like a deal to customers and beats mismo-coke margins,
   since softdrinks cost you ~PHP 12.7k/month in purchases.

g) June sales dropped 13% vs May. Watch July closely (first week
   is pacing at June's level, not May's). If the dip holds, the
   fastest levers are the weekday promo (e) and reactivating
   delivery — only 38 delivery orders ever, with a fee already
   built into the POS.
